## VivahSync: v1 Product Requirement

## Core Objective: Centralize guest communications, travel arrival/departure manifests, and real-time hotel check-ins into an actionable operational dashboard for on-ground hospitality teams.

## 1. Multi-Event RSVP & Guest Profiling

Sub-Event Granularity: Allow guests to RSVP for specific sub-events (e.g., Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding, Reception) rather than a simple binary event RSVP.

Party & Family Grouping: Group guests by "Party/Family Unit" under a single primary contact to allow one person to respond on behalf of their household.

Custom Metadata Capture: Collect critical logistical metadata during RSVP, including:

Dietary restrictions and allergies.

Special assistance/mobility requirements.

Plus-one approvals and child headcount.

Low-Friction Channels: Support RSVP collection via web links embedded in WhatsApp/SMS broadcasts, requiring no guest app download.

## 2. Travel Manifest & Flight/Train Tracking

Arrival & Departure Logging: Capture complete travel itineraries for each guest (Flight/Train number, arrival time, terminal/station, departure time).

Automated Logistics Manifests: Automatically group arriving guests into "Pickup Windows" based on landing times and destination hotels to streamline shuttle and cab dispatch.

Driver & Vehicle Fleet Mapping: Assign specific cabs, drivers, and fleet contacts to guest pickup slots, generating a live driver dispatch sheet.

Real-Time Delays & Statuses: Flag travel delays on the hospitality dashboard so transport teams can adjust pickup schedules dynamically.

## 3. Hotel Room Allocation, Check-in & Checkout

Room Block Matrix: Map reserved room blocks across multiple partner hotels against guest groups and arrival dates.

Desk Check-in / Checkout Console: Provide an optimized tablet view for hotel desk coordinators to:

Mark guests as Arrived at Airport, Checked-In, In-Room, or Checked-Out.

Track key handover and early check-in / late checkout requests.

Hamper & Welcome Kit Tracker: Link welcome kit distribution directly to the check-in event to ensure every party receives their hampers upon arrival.

## 4. Broadcast Messaging & Dynamic Digital Itineraries

Personalized Mobile Itineraries: Generate dynamic, web-based schedules for each guest showing their specific venue locations, shuttle pickup times, and dress codes.

Event Broadcast Alerts: Enable planners to send instant WhatsApp/SMS broadcasts for schedule updates, venue changes, or bus departure reminders (e.g., "Shuttles for the Sangeet leave the JW Marriott lobby in 15 minutes").

Live Attendance Desk: QR-code generation for guest passes to allow rapid scanning and check-in at high-security or venue-restricted events.
